# Metric Dictionary

## Customer Intelligence Analytics Platform

This document defines the governed business metrics used across the project.

The objective is to ensure that SQL analytics, Snowflake Semantic Views, Cortex Analyst, Cortex Agents, validation queries, and Streamlit use consistent metric definitions.

---

## 1. Total Customers

**Metric Name:** `TOTAL_CUSTOMERS`

**Business Definition:**  
Number of unique customers included in the observed customer snapshot.

**Formula:**  
`COUNT(DISTINCT CUSTOMER_ID)`

**Grain:**  
Snapshot or selected segment.

**Source:**  
`CORE.CUSTOMER_360_VIEW`

**Interpretation:**  
Represents the customer population included in the analysis.

**Limitations:**  
The available dataset represents a Q3 snapshot, not a historical monthly customer base.

---

## 2. Churned Customers

**Metric Name:** `CHURNED_CUSTOMERS`

**Business Definition:**  
Number of customers identified as churned in the observed snapshot.

**Formula:**  
`COUNT_IF(CHURN_FLAG = 1)`

**Grain:**  
Snapshot or selected segment.

**Source:**  
`CORE.CUSTOMER_360_VIEW`

**Interpretation:**  
Represents observed customer losses in the dataset.

---

## 3. Churn Rate

**Metric Name:** `CHURN_RATE`

**Business Definition:**  
Percentage of customers in the selected population identified as churned.

**Formula:**  
`CHURNED_CUSTOMERS / TOTAL_CUSTOMERS`

**Denominator:**  
All customers included in the selected snapshot or segment.

**Source:**  
`CORE.CUSTOMER_360_VIEW`

**Interpretation:**  
Measures the proportion of the observed customer population that churned.

**Limitations:**  
The denominator includes Stayed, Joined, and Churned customers because the dataset represents a single snapshot. This is not a historical monthly churn-rate calculation based on beginning-of-month active base.

---

## 4. Churn Contribution

**Metric Name:** `CHURN_CONTRIBUTION`

**Business Definition:**  
Share of total observed churn generated by a specific segment.

**Formula:**  
`CHURNED_CUSTOMERS_IN_SEGMENT / TOTAL_CHURNED_CUSTOMERS`

**Denominator:**  
All churned customers in the observed snapshot.

**Interpretation:**  
Shows where total churn volume is concentrated.

**Example:**  
A segment may have a moderate churn rate but still contribute a large share of total churn because of its size.

---

## 5. Churn Index

**Metric Name:** `CHURN_INDEX`

**Business Definition:**  
Relative churn rate of a segment compared with the overall churn rate.

**Formula:**  
`SEGMENT_CHURN_RATE / OVERALL_CHURN_RATE`

**Interpretation:**

- `1.00` = churn equals overall average
- `> 1.00` = churn above average
- `< 1.00` = churn below average

**Example:**  
A churn index of `1.50` means the segment churn rate is 50% higher than the overall churn rate.

---

## 6. Customers Reaching Tenure

**Metric Name:** `CUSTOMERS_REACHING_TENURE`

**Business Definition:**  
Number of customers whose observed tenure reached at least the specified tenure month.

**Formula:**  
`COUNT_IF(TENURE_IN_MONTHS >= TENURE_MONTH)`

**Grain:**  
Tenure month, optionally by segment.

**Interpretation:**  
Represents the approximate population exposed to churn at a given point in the customer lifecycle.

**Limitations:**  
Derived from a single snapshot and therefore does not represent a true historical cohort beginning-of-month base.

---

## 7. Churned at Tenure

**Metric Name:** `CHURNED_AT_TENURE`

**Business Definition:**  
Number of churned customers whose observed tenure ended at the specified tenure month.

**Formula:**  
`COUNT_IF(CHURN_FLAG = 1 AND TENURE_IN_MONTHS = TENURE_MONTH)`

**Grain:**  
Tenure month, optionally by segment.

---

## 8. Churn Rate at Tenure

**Metric Name:** `CHURN_RATE_AT_TENURE`

**Business Definition:**  
Observed churn rate among customers who reached a specific tenure month.

**Formula:**  
`CHURNED_AT_TENURE / CUSTOMERS_REACHING_TENURE`

**Denominator:**  
Customers whose observed tenure reached at least the selected tenure month.

**Grain:**  
Tenure month, optionally by customer, product, or commercial segment.

**Source:**  
`ANALYTICS.VW_CHURN_BY_TENURE` and `ANALYTICS.VW_CHURN_BY_TENURE_SEGMENT`

**Interpretation:**  
Provides the best available cross-sectional approximation of how churn behaves across the customer lifecycle using the current customer snapshot. It can be used to identify observed churn peaks by tenure and compare whether those peaks change across customer or product segments.

**Limitations:**  
This is not a historical cohort retention curve or a calendar-month churn rate. The dataset contains one customer snapshot rather than historical monthly snapshots.

`CHURN_RATE_AT_TENURE` should always be interpreted together with `CUSTOMERS_REACHING_TENURE`. At higher tenure months, smaller customer populations can make individual monthly churn observations more volatile.

Additional historical snapshots would be required to determine whether observed churn peaks are persistent over time and consistent across acquisition cohorts.

---

## 9. Customer Share

**Metric Name:** `CUSTOMER_SHARE`

**Business Definition:**  
Percentage of the relevant customer population belonging to a selected segment or adoption status.

**Formula:**  
`CUSTOMERS_IN_SEGMENT / TOTAL_CUSTOMERS_IN_RELEVANT_POPULATION`

**Interpretation:**  
Measures customer-base concentration.

---

## 10. Product Adoption Rate

**Metric Name:** `PRODUCT_ADOPTION_RATE`

**Business Definition:**  
Percentage of customers that have adopted a specific product or service.

**Formula:**  
`CUSTOMERS_WITH_PRODUCT / TOTAL_ELIGIBLE_CUSTOMERS`

**Interpretation:**  
Measures product penetration within the relevant population.

**Important:**  
Eligibility must be considered when a product is not applicable to every customer.

---

## 11. Total Service Count

**Metric Name:** `TOTAL_SERVICE_COUNT`

**Business Definition:**  
Number of core and add-on services associated with a customer.

**Components:**

Core:
- Phone Service
- Internet Service

Add-ons:
- Multiple Lines
- Online Security
- Online Backup
- Device Protection Plan
- Premium Tech Support
- Streaming TV
- Streaming Movies
- Streaming Music
- Unlimited Data

**Range:**  
1–11 services in the current dataset.

**Interpretation:**  
Used as a proxy for product-adoption depth.

**Limitations:**  
Service count does not imply equal economic or strategic value across products.

---

## 12. Average Monthly Charge

**Metric Name:** `AVG_MONTHLY_CHARGE`

**Business Definition:**  
Average recurring monthly charge across the selected customer population.

**Formula:**  
`AVG(MONTHLY_CHARGE)`

**Source:**  
Customer services data.

---

## 13. Total Monthly Charges

**Metric Name:** `TOTAL_MONTHLY_CHARGES`

**Business Definition:**  
Sum of current monthly charges across the selected customer population.

**Formula:**  
`SUM(MONTHLY_CHARGE)`

**Interpretation:**  
Represents the current monthly billing amount associated with the observed customer base.

---

## 14. Total Revenue

**Metric Name:** `TOTAL_REVENUE`

**Business Definition:**  
Total historical revenue recorded for the selected customer population.

**Formula:**  
`SUM(TOTAL_REVENUE)`

**Source:**  
Source-provided revenue field.

**Important:**  
This is accumulated historical revenue, not current-period revenue.

---

## 15. Churned Monthly Revenue

**Metric Name:** `CHURNED_MONTHLY_REVENUE`

**Business Definition:**  
Sum of monthly charges associated with customers identified as churned.

**Formula:**  
`SUM(MONTHLY_CHARGE WHERE CHURN_FLAG = 1)`

**Interpretation:**  
Represents recurring monthly charges associated with customers who have already churned.

**Important:**  
This is not called `Revenue at Risk` because the customers have already churned.

---

## 16. Churned Historical Revenue

**Metric Name:** `CHURNED_HISTORICAL_REVENUE`

**Business Definition:**  
Historical revenue generated by customers identified as churned.

**Formula:**  
`SUM(TOTAL_REVENUE WHERE CHURN_FLAG = 1)`

**Interpretation:**  
Shows the historical economic significance of churned customers.

**Limitations:**  
Does not represent future revenue lost.

---

## 17. Revenue Share

**Metric Name:** `REVENUE_SHARE`

**Business Definition:**  
Share of total historical revenue associated with a selected customer segment.

**Formula:**  
`SEGMENT_TOTAL_REVENUE / TOTAL_REVENUE`

**Interpretation:**  
Measures economic concentration across customer segments.

---

## 18. Average CLTV

**Metric Name:** `AVG_CLTV`

**Business Definition:**  
Average source-provided Customer Lifetime Value score within a selected population.

**Formula:**  
`AVG(CLTV)`

**Important:**  
The CLTV calculation methodology is not provided by the source dataset. This project does not recalculate CLTV.

**Interpretation:**  
Used as a relative customer-value indicator rather than an independently validated financial valuation.

---

## 19. Customer Value Segment

**Metric Name:** `CUSTOMER_VALUE_SEGMENT`

**Business Definition:**  
Customer-value segmentation based on quartiles of the source-provided CLTV field.

**Method:**

- Q1 → Low Value
- Q2 → Mid-Low Value
- Q3 → Mid-High Value
- Q4 → High Value

**Calculation:**  
`NTILE(4) OVER (ORDER BY CLTV)`

**Limitations:**  
Customers with identical CLTV values may fall into adjacent quartiles because NTILE distributes rows rather than unique values.

---

## 20. Average Tenure

**Metric Name:** `AVG_TENURE_MONTHS`

**Business Definition:**  
Average observed customer tenure in months.

**Formula:**  
`AVG(TENURE_IN_MONTHS)`

---

## 21. Average Satisfaction Score

**Metric Name:** `AVG_SATISFACTION_SCORE`

**Business Definition:**  
Average source-provided satisfaction score across the selected customer population.

**Formula:**  
`AVG(SATISFACTION_SCORE)`

**Important Analytical Limitation:**  
Satisfaction shows an unusually strong relationship with churn in this dataset. It should be treated cautiously in predictive modeling because it may contain post-outcome information or target leakage.

# Analytical Principles

The project follows several analytical rules:

1. **Rate is not volume.**  
   A segment can have a high churn rate but low business impact because its population is small.

2. **Volume is not financial impact.**  
   Churn contribution and revenue exposure should be considered separately.

3. **Association is not causation.**  
   Product adoption, contract type, satisfaction, CLTV, and other attributes may be associated with churn without causing it.

4. **Observed churn is not future churn risk.**  
   `CHURNED_MONTHLY_REVENUE` describes customers who already churned. `Revenue at Risk` should only be used if a future predictive model estimates churn risk among active customers.

5. **Lifecycle churn is not cohort retention.**  
   `CHURN_RATE_AT_TENURE` is a cross-sectional approximation based on the available Q3 snapshot. The dataset does not contain the historical monthly customer snapshots required to construct true cohort retention curves.

6. **AI must use governed metrics.**  
   Cortex Analyst and Cortex Agents should answer quantitative questions using these approved definitions rather than generating new metric logic.
